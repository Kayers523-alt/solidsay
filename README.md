# SolidSay — Offline 3D Model & Thermal Design Studio

**Try it live (free, no sign-up): https://kayers523-alt.github.io/solidsay/**

Type plain words, get a printable 3D model. Built for chips, boards, heatsinks and enclosures — with a built-in thermal solver and DC IR-drop check.

## What it does

- Turns words into 3D models — microchip packages, circuit boards, cooling parts, enclosures
- 50 hardware presets, including an AMD MI300X 750W chiplet mockup
- Thermal solver with heatmap, plus an electrical solver (IR-drop, rail voltage, copper swaps)
- Exports printable 3D files — everything runs 100% on your device
- No cloud, no account, no upload. Your design never leaves your browser.

## Honest scope

Thermal numbers are a fast design guide, not CFD/FEA sign-off. There is no measured validation yet — use them to compare designs and catch obvious heat problems, not as engineering certification.

## Run it

No install. Open the live link above, pick a preset, type what you want, and export.

Found a bug or a wrong number? Open an issue — the thermal and electrical solvers are the parts that matter most, so call out anything that looks off.

If it helps you, star the repo. It is the simplest way to tell other hardware builders it exists.
